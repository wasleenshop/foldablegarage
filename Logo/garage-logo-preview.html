"W-Shape Pergola" — A premium architectural mark.
W-shaped roof profile with two peaks, horizontal rafters, decorative grill/lattice work, and a rich gold-to-copper gradient for a luxury Middle Eastern aesthetic.

      Rafters  →  Horizontal roof support beams

      Gold     →  Premium UAE luxury aesthetic
